## Tokens: invalidate observed stale metadata

`Tokens` retains device, inode, observed size, mtime and ctime with each cached transcript. It resets on identity change, shrinkage relative to the observed file size, or timestamp change at the same size. This catches replacement, same-size edits and shrinkage in an incomplete trailing record without changing ordinary append, line-boundary or serialized-read behavior.

The six direct Codex rollout fixtures cover same-size atomic replacement, changed-mtime rewrite, preserved-mtime/changed-ctime rewrite, larger atomic replacement, partial-tail shrink and append/incomplete/concurrent controls. The two real owner-RPC fixtures call `sessions.tokens` and `cards.tokens` in both orders, refresh 100 to 250, repeat the reads and verify resumed-thread deduplication. They close the prior alpha.17 cached 100/fresh 250 observation for those metadata changes. The shared reader also serves Claude paths, but these new rewrite fixtures are Codex; existing Claude append/subagent/shrink neighbors remain historical coverage.

No freshness guarantee is made for a writer that preserves every observed stamp, rewrites a prefix while growing the same file, or races the read. No real provider, automatic renderer refresh or polling-latency claim follows from these owned calls.

## Board: marker coordinates and durable cancellation

The marker's destination index was computed after removing the dragged card, but rendered into the displayed list that still contained it. The fix maps that index back into the displayed list when dragging within the same column. It changes marker placement only; card movement, persistence and animation are unchanged.

The exact 3,726-byte added sweep block was extracted and executed against separately built original and corrected Board sources. Each theme drives four trusted browser mouse gestures: forward, reverse, no-op and end. It checks marker placement, Escape clearing, unchanged status/rank and four matching trusted dragstart/dragend pairs with no drop. Original forward/end positions were wrong and no-op had no marker, producing six intended failures overall; reverse controls passed. The corrected block passes all eight. These are cancellation cases, so they establish no persisted move.

A separate green worker actually drops forward, reverse and across columns in both themes. It asserts trusted dragstart/drop, actual stored and displayed order, marker clearance and natural finite animation completion within the bounded wait. The settled orders are B/A/C, A/C/B and A/D/B/C respectively. Cross-column replacement can detach the source mount before document-level dragend; that event is not required there, while trusted start/drop and both orders remain required. Two additional controls per theme check a capped Done column and empty target with cancellation only. No animation was forcibly finished or cancelled to take settled screenshots.

The historical before probe already performed six drops with correct stored order and 18 reviewed images, but its post-drop captures could occur during motion. They are not settled-layout proof. Its forward mid-drag images do show the wrong marker and are paired below with the corrected marker and freshly settled images. The eight fresh durable before/after images are cancellation states, not pictures of the mid-drag defect.
