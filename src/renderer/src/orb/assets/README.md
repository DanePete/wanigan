Captured high dynamic range lighting for the glass and water reflections.

`room.png` — a generated, room-only backdrop made for Wanigan (it was the Mission
room backdrop in Wanigan 1). It contains no orb, face, interface or fluid. The
optics trace rays through the glass and the simulated water onto it as a plane
behind the vessel, so the room is what you see refracted.

Neither is a rendered orb or a baked animation. Both ship with Wanigan and are
loaded same-origin; no remote request is made when the orb runs.
